Research Interests

Five connected research directions, from geometric network structure to efficient algorithms and temporal models.

01 / Models and structure

Network Geometry

How spatial structure and degree heterogeneity shape random networks.
02 / Local structure, fluctuations

Subgraphs and Rare Events

Subgraph statistics, optimal patterns and rare fluctuations.
03 / Detection and recovery

Inference of Hidden Structures

Homophily, network inference and recovery of latent patterns.
04 / Efficient computation

Scalable Graph Algorithms

Efficient generation, sampling and analysis of random networks.
05 / Network processes

Temporal Networks & Dynamics

Connectivity and stochastic processes in time-varying graphs.
