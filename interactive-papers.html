THE INTUITION

What is a clique?

Imagine four people who all know each other. In a network, this is a clique: every pair of nodes is connected. Pick any two people in that group: you will always find a direct connection between them. No links are missing.

THE FORMALISM

What is a clique?

Let $G=(V,E)$ be a simple undirected graph. A subset $S\subseteq V$ is a clique when every pair of its vertices is joined by an edge.

\[ \left|E(G[S])\right|=\binom{|S|}{2} \]

For four vertices, this means exactly $\binom{4}{2}=6$ edges.
