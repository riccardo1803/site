RICCARDO MICHIELAN

A little about me.

I'm a mathematician working at the intersection of probability, random graphs, and complex networks. My academic journey began at the University of Padua, where I studied Mathematics, and continued at the University of Twente, where I completed my PhD in 2025, investigating how geometry influences the structure of complex networks.

I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at the Gran Sasso Science Institute, working on graph algorithms and network models within the GAMING project. I have also been awarded a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellowship for PAiNe — Planted Anomalies in Networks — at the University of Padua, starting in February 2027.
